How to read this

Fire-active cell-days. The lower 48 is cut into 0.1 degree cells (590 by 260). A cell counts on a day if at least one 1 km pixel was classed as fire. A week adds up its days (the last week of a year is scaled to 7 days).

Why harmonize. MODIS (2000-2011) is less sensitive to small fires than VIIRS (2012 on), so raw counts jump in 2012 for reasons that have nothing to do with fire. Harmonization uses the years both sensors flew, 2012-2026, to learn the probability that VIIRS S-NPP would have flagged a cell, given what MODIS Terra and Aqua saw. Before 2012 the harmonized value is that expected count of VIIRS-equivalent cells (2003-2011; Aqua starts in 2002, so 2000-2002 are blank). From 2012 it is the real VIIRS count, so the record has no step.

Not observed. Cloud, smoke or no satellite pass hide the ground. Hatched weeks had a clear view of less than 35% of the region's land cells, so a low count there is not evidence of little fire. Fire counts are only compared where the satellite looked. VIIRS S-NPP data begin on 2012-01-19.

Normal and percentile. The grey band is the 10th to 90th percentile of each week over 2003-2025 (harmonized or raw as selected), the line its median. A percentile of 80 means 2026's week was busier than 80% of the same weeks in those 23 years.

Sources. NASA LAADS DAAC: MODIS Terra MOD14A1 and Aqua MYD14A1 (collection 6.1 daily thermal anomalies, 1 km), VIIRS S-NPP VNP14A1 and NOAA-20 VJ114A1 (collection 2). The harmonization model is described in MODELLING_LOG.md. Daily layers are served from /data/history/. Data through 2026-11-12. Days with no downloaded granule are blank rather than zero; a week needs at least 3 days of data to be shown, and annual totals for years with gaps are scaled to a full year.
